# Can I Write Your Name on My Heart? ♡

A warm, candlelit, storybook romantic web experience built around the aesthetic:
> **Cozy moonlit bedroom × handmade love story × kawaii cat × warm fairy lights × soft pink romance**

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Color Palette

Designed specifically around a warm amber, cocoa, and cream atmosphere rather than generic pastel pink:

- **Warm Cream** (`#FFF3E4`): Main background
- **Soft Vanilla** (`#FFE8D2`): Soft card surface
- **Blush Beige** (`#F7D5C4`): Warm surface & blush
- **Dusty Rose** (`#D98282`): Primary romantic accent
- **Soft Coral** (`#E99A91`): Romantic coral
- **Rosewood** (`#A8545C`): Deep romantic velvet
- **Cocoa Brown** (`#63443D`): Warm wood & subtext
- **Espresso** (`#3D2927`): Deep reading text (never harsh black)
- **Candle Glow** (`#FFD98A`): Fairy lights & stars
- **Midnight Blue** (`#18233D`): Night sky chapter

---

## ✨ Features & Chapters

1. **🕯️ Chapter 1: The Moonlit Bedroom (Hero)**
   - Layered parallax scene with master artwork.
   - **Interactive Cat Mascot**:
     - 2-level cursor tracking: eyes track fast, head tilts smoothly.
     - Blushes when cursor gets close (< 140px).
     - Idles: blinks at ~3s, ear twitches at ~7s, glances at sign at ~10s.
     - Click cat to hear a purr/meow and spawn floating hearts!
   - Micro-flickering fairy lights & swaying ivy vines.
   - Handcrafted wooden sign with pulsing heart: *"Can I write your name on my heart?"*
   - Delicate cursor trail with occasional mini hearts.

2. **🌷 Chapter 2: Before I Ask... (Interactive Bookshelf)**
   - Interactive 3D books from the reference photo:
     - *Good People*
     - *Softer Days*
     - *Brighter You*
     - *Happier Us*
     - *Forever ♡*
     - *More Love*, *More Smiles*, *More Us*, *Always You ♡*
   - Click any book to pull it out in 3D and read the handwritten note inside with sound effect.
   - Glowing cat night lamp and shelf mascot.

3. **💌 Chapter 3: The Letter (Candlelit Desk)**
   - Candlelit desk with flickering flame.
   - Wax sealed envelope: click the heart seal to break the seal and unfold the handwritten letter.
   - Dedicated letter for Khushi.

4. **🌙 Chapter 4: Night Scene (Under the Moonlit Sky)**
   - Palette transition to Midnight Blue (`#18233D`) and gold stars.
   - Interactive star constellations: hover/click stars to connect constellations and reveal secret star whispers.
   - Shooting stars zipping across the midnight sky.
   - Cat & Bunny silhouettes wrapped in a blanket under the moon.

5. **❤️ Chapter 5: The Heart (The Little Question)**
   - Giant handcrafted beating heart with rhythmic acoustic pulse.
   - Click to hear heartbeat sound effect.

6. **📝 Chapter 6: The Final Question & Escaping NO Button**
   - Handcrafted wooden card matching the hero sign.
   - **Escaping NO button**: dodges cursor or tap playfully with springy evasion!
   - Playful cat remarks ("Oops! Too fast!", "Error 404: NO not found!", etc.).
   - Each attempt makes the **[ YES ♡ ]** button grow larger and more magnetic.

7. **💥 Grand Finale: The Celebration**
   - Fullscreen heart confetti and golden sparkles explosion.
   - Celebration fanfare sound.
   - Radiant inscription: **❤️ KHUSHI ❤️** (*"There. It belongs here. Always and forever. ♡"*).
   - Official Love Certificate keepsake card (with Save/Print option).

8. **🎵 Cozy Lofi Soundtrack**
   - Zero-dependency Web Audio API synthesizer generating warm Rhodes/kalimba chords with vinyl warmth.
   - Floating audio widget in top corner to toggle music on/off.

## 🚀 Running the Project

### Development Server:
```bash
npm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/) in your browser.

### Production Build:
```bash
npm run build
```
Built files will be created in `dist/`.
